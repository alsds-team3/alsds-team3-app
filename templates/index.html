About locationIQ

locationIQ — AI-Assisted Location Decision Support System is a Worcester-focused site-feasibility tool built for the Urban Analytics AI Consultancy capstone.

We combine an Azure SQL backend of Worcester POIs, CBG centroids, and local business data with the locationIQ advisor that checks each site, compares candidate locations, and explains results in plain language for non-technical owners.

What you can do

  • Drop a candidate pin anywhere in the Worcester service area.
  • Pick a calibrated business category — or type a plain label like "bakery".
  • Compare alternative locations side-by-side (same NAICS only).
  • Ask the chatbot follow-up questions about competitors and limits.
